# Third-party components

The offline engine is [whisper.cpp v1.9.2](https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp), licensed under MIT. The Tiny English and Base English [Whisper models](https://github.com/openai/whisper) are licensed under MIT and distributed in [GGML format](https://huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp). The setup script downloads the runtime and weights and checks their SHA-256 hashes.
